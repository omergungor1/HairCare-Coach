## 1. Executive Summary & Page Objectives

The primary objective of the HairCare Coach landing page is **Demand Validation & Smoke Testing (Phase 1 Validation)** before committing mobile app development budget.

The refreshed landing page is built strictly around the **FINAL APPROVED Brand Kit**:
1. **Validate Willingness-to-Pay:** Measure direct commercial intent for the Early Supporter Pro tier ($2.99/mo or $29.99/yr) vs. Free tier.
2. **Capture 500+ Qualified Waitlist Leads:** High-intent subscribers from Reddit (r/tressless), YouTube men's grooming communities, and dermatological forums.
3. **Establish Category Leadership in "Honest Habit Science":** Visually and textually differentiate from predatory "snake-oil" supplement ads by adopting an uncompromising, evidence-based stance.
4. **Enforce Approved Visual Identity:** Deep Forest Emerald (`#0F3A2E`) authority, Vibrant Mint (`#10B981`) conversion momentum, Clean Off-White (`#F8FAFC`) editorial clarity, and "The Vital Loop" geometric logo mark.
