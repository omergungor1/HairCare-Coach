## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Does HairCare Coach promise to regrow my hair?
**A:** No. Anyone promising guaranteed hair regrowth in an app is being dishonest. Hair regrowth depends entirely on your genetics, biological cause of hair loss, and the specific clinically proven treatments you and your physician choose to use. **What HairCare Coach does guarantee** is that you will stay remarkably consistent with whichever routine you follow, track your photographic changes without distortion, and never miss an application.

### Q2: Is scalp massage a scientifically proven cure for baldness?
**A:** No, it is not a standalone cure. Small pilot studies indicate scalp massage can temporarily increase localized blood flow and mechanical stretch in dermal papilla cells, but it cannot reverse advanced androgenetic alopecia on its own. In our app, scalp massage is treated as a relaxing, stress-reducing wellness companion practice—not a miracle substitute for proven therapies.

### Q3: Where are my sensitive progress photos stored?
**A:** Your photos remain on your personal smartphone storage by default. We do not upload your scalp photos to our servers or use them to train public AI models. Your privacy and medical confidentiality come first.

### Q4: Can I use HairCare Coach if I've had a hair transplant?
**A:** Absolutely. Post-transplant care requires strict adherence to washing schedules, scabbing protocols, and medication timing across the crucial first 12 months. HairCare Coach provides customized timelines to help you protect your investment.

### Q5: When will the app launch?
**A:** We are currently opening early access to our private beta for the first 500 waitlist supporters. You will receive an invitation via email as soon as beta builds are live on TestFlight (iOS) and Google Play Beta (Android).
